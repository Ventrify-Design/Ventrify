Quality Control

Gates, milestones, and transparency

Clear checkpoints ensure alignment and protect both parties throughout the engagement.

Client Gates
Gate 1 — After Research
All research findings presented in a comprehensive deck. Client reviews market analysis, competitor landscape, and user insights. Sign-off required before Define phase begins.
Gate 2 — After Define
Product vision, personas, feature scope, and roadmap presented. Client signs the Statement of Work. Scope locks at this point — all remaining phases execute against the agreed specification.
Payment Milestones
40% — Upfront
Paid at project kickoff. Covers the Discover and Define phases, including all research and strategic planning.
30% — Design Start
Paid after Gate 2 approval and SOW signature. Unlocks Design and Development phases.
30% — On Delivery
Final payment upon completed handoff, app store submission, and IP transfer.

No work proceeds until the corresponding gate is approved and milestone is paid.
