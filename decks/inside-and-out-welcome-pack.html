Quality Gates

Your sign-off points

Gate 1: Research Sign-Off
After Phase 1, we present all research findings in a comprehensive deck. You review market analysis, competitor landscape, and user insights. We proceed only after your written approval.
Gate 2: Scope Lock
After Phase 2, we present product vision, personas, features, and roadmap. You sign the Statement of Work. This locks the MVP scope — everything from here is built to this agreed specification.
Changes to Must Have features after Gate 2 require a formal change request with timeline and cost impact assessment.
