The Challenge

Debt is one of the most stressful, emotionally loaded problems people face — yet every tool built to solve it looks and feels like a spreadsheet. Dry. Passive. Easy to ignore. The founder came to us with a sharper take: what if managing debt felt less like accounting and more like training?

The opportunity was real. Millions of people carry debt but lack the motivation or structure to tackle it consistently. Generic budgeting apps weren't cutting through. DebtFit needed to be something people actually wanted to open — a product that made progress visible, rewarding, and genuinely engaging.

Our Approach

The fitness metaphor wasn't just a brand device — it became the product's entire logic. We used it to reframe every interaction: overpayments became "workouts," interest reduction became "calories burned," milestones earned XP. Discovery confirmed that the emotional triggers driving gym consistency — streaks, progress bars, visible wins — translate directly to financial behaviour change.

From there, we designed a system that tracks debt across connected accounts, generates personalised payoff strategies, and surfaces progress in ways that feel rewarding rather than clinical. Every screen was built to answer one question: does this make the user feel like they're winning?

What We Built

A complete venture build — brand identity, design system, and full iOS application. Every layer designed and built under one roof, from the DF mark through to the gamified debt-tracking experience. Intelligent tracking across multiple debt sources, personalised payoff strategies, gamified progress through XP, streaks, and performance summaries — and a marketing website to bring it all to market.

A product people actually come back to.
