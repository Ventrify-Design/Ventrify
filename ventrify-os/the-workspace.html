Action Queue

Your work this week,
across every program.

The queue is opinionated about what needs you next.

Gates ready to close. Cards drafted by agents and awaiting your review before they reach the founder. Stuck founders who haven’t engaged in days. QA flags from ux-designer or source-verifier.

Three urgency tiers. High urgency surfaces gates the founder has signed off and stuck founders — the kind of thing that breaks momentum if you miss it.

  • Aggregated across every program your team runs
  • Coloured urgency stripes, named action types, one-click resolution
  • Permanent audit trail of who closed what, when
  • Compounds into operator KPIs across cohorts (v2)

Team & Settings

Scale by operator.
Govern by licence.

Multi-operator orgs · tier-bound capacity.

Studio, Cohort, and Enterprise tiers all run multiple operators. The Workspace shows seats used vs. seats available, programs assigned per operator, role-based permissions.

Settings cover the licence (tier, renewal date, programs running), success contact (Cohort+), billing (procurement-friendly invoicing), and integrations (the Studio, engagement repos, Stripe).

  • Operator seats and program limits enforced per tier
