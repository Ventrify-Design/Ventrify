FAQ

Common questions

How does annual licensing work?
You pick a tier, sign a one-year licence, and get unlimited programs within that tier’s operator and program limits. No per-engagement fees. Renew or upgrade annually. Procurement-friendly invoicing on Cohort and Enterprise tiers.
Which tier is right for us?
Boutique studios and individual operators — Starter. Venture studios and small accelerators — Studio. Accelerators or incubators running cohort-style programs — Cohort. Corporate innovation labs and procurement-driven institutions — Enterprise. Book a demo and we’ll size it with you.
Can we white-label the platform?
Yes — on the Enterprise tier. White-label includes your branding, your domain for the program portal, and configuration to match your operating model. Available as an add-on on Cohort by exception.
Who owns the work the platform produces?
Your program owns every output of every venture. Code, brand assets, decks, research, design system — all delivered to the founder under your program’s arrangement. Ventrify retains no rights to the venture outputs.
What does onboarding look like?
Starter: self-serve setup with documentation and community support. Cohort: dedicated success contact and operator certification within the first two weeks, embedded onboarding. Enterprise: embedded onboarding, SLA, and a customised configuration cycle.
How do you stop the AI from hallucinating?
The quality substrate. Every agent output passes through deterministic tools first — qa:lexicon catches off-voice writing, qa:consistency catches mismatched figures across files, qa:investor-readiness adversarially scans the rendered pitch deck. Tools do the deterministic checks; agents apply judgement on what to do about the results. The fleet’s immune system is agent-output-validator, a mandatory post-hook on every agent run.
How is this different from hiring an agency?
An agency engagement is project-priced, headcount-bound, and 6–9 months long — one venture at a time. Licensing the platform converts that delivery capacity into infrastructure your own team owns. Same outputs, fixed annual cost, repeatable across every program in your portfolio.
