Licensing
How much does Ventrify OS cost? +

Annual licences in three tiers: Starter $4,800/yr, Cohort (contact us for pricing), Enterprise $150,000+/yr. All prices in USD. No per-engagement fees within your tier — every venture earns the full 70+ deliverable scope.

Which tier should we choose? +

Tier on operators and program volume: Starter (1 operator, up to 6 programs/yr), Cohort (up to 4 operators, up to 24 programs/yr, dedicated success contact), Enterprise (unlimited operators and programs, white-label, SLA, embedded onboarding). Book a demo and we’ll size it with you.

Are there per-engagement fees? +

No. The annual licence covers unlimited programs within your tier’s operator and program limits. Predictable economics — you can model the cost of every venture before it starts.

How does billing work? +

Annual invoicing in USD, payable on 30-day terms. Cohort and Enterprise tiers are procurement-friendly: PO numbers, vendor onboarding, and quarterly billing on request.

What’s included in platform updates? +

Every licence gets ongoing platform improvements as part of the annual fee — new agents, new tools, new deliverable types, performance and quality upgrades. No separate version purchases. Our own reference programmes feed product improvements back into every licensee’s build.

Onboarding
How long does onboarding take? +

Days, not months. Self-serve setup on Starter (typically a week including operator certification). Cohort: dedicated success contact and operator certification within the first two weeks. Enterprise: embedded onboarding and a customised configuration cycle.

What does operator certification involve? +

A short structured programme that walks an operator through a complete venture — brief intake, gate reviews, agent orchestration, QA validation, founder handoff. Most operators certify in 2–4 days. Once certified, they can run programs end-to-end on the platform.

Can our first venture start during onboarding? +

Yes. Most licensees pair onboarding with a real first venture — the operator certifies on a live brief. The platform is fully self-supporting from day one; the dedicated success contact (Cohort and Enterprise) shadows the first program for safety.

What technology do our operators need? +

A modern browser for the Workspace (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). For the deeper agent runs: VS Code, Claude Code, Node.js 18+, Git, and Figma Desktop. All free or near-free. Mac, Windows, or Linux supported. Enterprise tier includes optional managed environments if your security policy restricts local installs.

What if onboarding hits a blocker? +

Cohort and Enterprise tiers include direct access to a dedicated success contact who unblocks issues within hours. Starter uses email support — hello@ventrify.io, 48-hour response.

For Your Operators
Do our operators need to be developers? +

No. Operators orchestrate; AI agents execute. Comfort with a terminal and a code editor is enough. The platform handles every specialist task — research, design, build, brand — via agents. Operators review work at the gates and run the founder relationship.

What does the “one operator” leverage actually mean? +

A traditional product design agency needs 8–12 specialists per venture. Ventrify OS replaces those specialists with AI agents working inside one orchestrated workflow. Your single operator gains the delivery capacity of an entire agency — without growing headcount.

What does an operator’s day-to-day look like? +

Operators log into the Workspace at ventrify.io/workspace. Daily flow: skim the action queue (gates to close, cards to review, stuck founders to nudge), drill into the program detail view for any venture that needs attention, jump into the the Studio for that program when the founder needs a hand. Most operators run 4–6 active programs in parallel.

What if an agent gets something wrong? +

Several layers catch issues before founders see them. source-verifier fact-checks every citation in Research. ux-designer runs three audit rounds on every MVP. The data room’s L1 layer surfaces strategic forks as cards before they get baked into deeper work. And operators always review and approve at the three formal gates. Mistakes get caught early, not after a founder reads 8,000 words.

Can we customise the platform for our program model? +

Yes. Cohort and Enterprise tiers include configuration of phase order, deliverable specs, brand kit defaults, and gate criteria to match your program model. Enterprise also includes white-label and bespoke agent extensions.

Programs & Deliverables
What does each program produce? +

70+ deliverables — mostly live, not PDFs. Each venture ships seven portal hubs as three-layer data rooms (Research, Vision, Strategy, Financials, Marketing, Video, Blog), a per-engagement design system site, a working MVP for iOS and Android with DEMO_MODE, a 9-page marketing website, and a focused PDF set (Investor Pitch Deck, SOW, Welcome Pack). See the showcase for examples and the data room page for how the hubs work.

Are deliverables fully branded for each founder? +

Yes. Brand kit intake captures each founder’s colours, fonts, logo, and tone. Every deliverable uses these — including 37 auto-sourced brand photos that match the venture’s industry and style.

How long does a program take? +

6–16 weeks from brief to launched venture. Some phases run in parallel, which is what makes the speed possible. Discovery sprints can compress this to 2 weeks; full launch with post-launch retainer can extend to 14–16 weeks.

How many programs can run in parallel? +

Per-tier program limits, but per-operator we typically see 4–6 active programs running simultaneously without quality drop. Cohort and Enterprise tiers remove the program cap entirely.

What if a founder needs something the platform doesn’t cover? +

The platform covers 70+ standard deliverables for the typical pre-seed and seed venture. For bespoke needs, your operator can extend the workflow themselves, or Enterprise customers can request platform-level extensions added to the roadmap.

Procurement & IP
Who owns the work the platform produces? +

Your program owns every output of every venture — code, brand assets, decks, research, design system — all delivered to the founder under your program’s arrangement. Ventrify retains no rights to the venture outputs.

Can we white-label the platform? +

Yes — on the Enterprise tier. White-label includes your branding, your domain for the program portal, and configuration to match your operating model. Available as an add-on on Cohort by exception.

What about data residency, security, and SLA? +

Enterprise tier includes contractual SLA, data-handling commitments, and configurable deployment to meet procurement and security requirements. We support managed environments for institutions whose policy restricts external SaaS. Talk to us for specifics.

What happens to our work if we don’t renew? +

Every venture output is yours from the moment it’s produced — it lives on your infrastructure, in your repositories, on your the Studio. If a licence ends, the platform stops accepting new ventures, but everything already delivered remains entirely yours.
