The Challenge

Debt is stressful. Every tool built to solve it looks like a spreadsheet.

Debt is one of the most emotionally loaded problems people face — yet every tool built to address it feels dry, passive, and easy to ignore. The founder came to us with a sharper take: what if managing debt felt less like accounting and more like training?

The opportunity was real. Millions of people carry debt but lack the motivation or structure to tackle it consistently. Generic budgeting apps weren’t cutting through. DebtFit needed to be something people actually wanted to open — a product that made progress visible, rewarding, and genuinely engaging.

Phase 1 · Phase 2 · The Data Room

The fitness metaphor became the product’s entire logic.

Phase 1 Discover produced the Research Hub L3 (fully cited, source-verified). The metaphor surfaced as a Vision Hub Provocation Card: treat overpayments as “workouts,” interest reduction as “calories burned,” milestones as XP — gym-consistency triggers translate to financial behaviour change. The founder resolved the card; Gate 2 closed with the metaphor as the product’s entire mental model.

From there, information-architect mapped 58 screens to the founder-resolved cards. backend-architect defined the Firestore schema for connected-account debt tracking before app code was written. The platform produced a system that tracks debt across accounts, generates personalised payoff strategies, and surfaces progress as a workout history.
