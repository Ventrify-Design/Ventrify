The Data Room

Every program hub on Ventrify OS renders as a three-layer data room. The engagement gates on founders resolving questions — not on weeks of deck revisions. This is how a 10-person agency’s output ships in 6–16 weeks.

Traditional venture studios and agencies ship giant decks. The founder reads (maybe), emails feedback, the agency revises, the cycle takes weeks. This produces revisions, not decisions. Ventrify OS replaces it with an architecture that forces engagement at the point of decision.

Architecture

Three layers per hub.
One way to engage.

Research, Vision, Strategy, Financials — every program hub in the Studio uses the same three-layer structure. Founders see the questions first. The depth is available when they want it.

L1 — Default view

Provocation Cards

5–7 strategic forks per hub. Surprising findings, hidden insights, assumption flags, open questions. Each card is one question the founder must engage with — not a paragraph to skim.

The Playbook

How a hub gets built.

Seven steps from raw brief to gate sign-off. Same sequence every time, every hub.

01

AI research agents write L3

3,000–8,000 words per hub. Every claim sourced, every number cited. Hubs are produced in parallel by domain-specific agents — research-curator, source-verifier, financial-modeler — depending on the phase.

02

Cards drafted from L3

The research-curator agent reads L3 and drafts 5–7 Provocation Cards. Each card distils a strategic fork or surfaces an assumption hidden in the research that needs the founder’s judgement.

03

Founder reviews L1 in the Studio

The founder opens the hub and sees only the cards. Each card is one question. They comment inline, agree, pick a direction, or ask for more.

04

Cards resolved

Every card is closed by founder action — agreement, a chosen direction, or a deliberate ‘won’t fix’. Permanent audit trail in git.

05

L3 rebuilt against decisions

If material strategic changes came out of the cards, the underlying research document is rebuilt to reflect the resolved direction. L3 always represents the current state of the engagement.

06

L3 published for founder review

The ‘View source documents’ expander unlocks. The founder can now read the full research aligned with the decisions they’ve made — not the version that existed before they engaged.

07

Gate sign-off in the Studio

Single click in the hub. STATUS.md auto-updates in the engagement repo. The next phase unlocks for your operator. No calendar invite. No follow-up email.

The Sign-off Loop

Gates close themselves.

Every gate in the engagement — Gate 1 (after Discover), Gate 2 (scope lock), Gate 2.5 (financials) — closes via the same in-portal loop. Operator workflow is unblocked the moment the founder clicks sign-off.

Safety Rails

The Studio protects the loop.

Two enforcement rules sit inside the Studio itself. Operators can’t accidentally skip the sequence — the Studio won’t let them.

L3 stays hidden until current

The ‘View source documents’ expander is hidden from the founder until L3 has been rebuilt against the resolved cards. Founders never review stale work — the depth they read is always aligned with the decisions they’ve made.

Sign-off button stays disabled

The gate sign-off button is greyed out until every card is resolved AND L3 is current. A gate can’t close prematurely — the engagement can’t skip a decision the founder hasn’t made.
