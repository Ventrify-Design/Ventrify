The third graphic

The dial says 80. Nothing on screen says where the 80 came from — which of the seven categories carried it and which held it back.

All three sit in the wider-column arrangement, with the band chip dropped into the gauge's opening so the dial keeps its size. Each bar is coloured by its own strength rather than the overall tone, so a weak category shows red even on an 80 — that is the whole point of putting it here. Driven straight off S.categories, so it works for any assessment and any number of categories. Shown at 80 and at 55; switch brands to check both.
