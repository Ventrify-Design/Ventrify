Rows without strokes

Three zebra treatments for the engagement list, on the real data and the real markup.

The defect, measured. It isn't overlap — the list draws two different divider tokens. Group captions use outline-variant (white at 10%); data rows use surface-container-highest (a solid grey, #3B3F43). Two strokes of different colour and apparent weight alternate down one list, and where a group ends the two sit one pixel apart. Zebra removes the question entirely — there is nothing to misalign.
