Blockers— resolve before any wire
1
Reprice the round
$60M pre is Series-A pricing on a seed-stage asset; comps and three valuation methods cap defensible value at $20–35M pre. No wire at $60M pre.
2
Reconcile the financial model
One version shows +$4.25M cash trough, another −$1.94M; break-even is stated as M24, M32 and M35; trial-to-paid is 20% (deck) vs 35% (model). Establish that $20M actually funds to the milestones.
3
Verify the ilumoni track record + co-founder roles
The whole traction case rests on Monely Ltd (dissolved 2024); confirm Lancaster’s and Goudge’s formal roles (absent from Companies House) and obtain raw, population-weighted PoC metrics.
Key— resolve in diligence
4
Get a credible Cleo-differentiation answer
Cleo (~7M users, $280M+ ARR, net-profitable) already occupies the “AI money coach” category the deck omits; without a defensible distinction the core thesis is exposed.
5
Secure a named ML / data lead
The four-engine moat is unbuilt and no current team member is verified to build it; the build is the differentiation.
6
Pressure-test CAC compression + distribution
Independent validation that a 0.4–0.6 viral coefficient and 70% organic mix are achievable in the US within 25 months; the deck calls it “a hypothesis, not a guarantee.”
Standard— confirm on the call
7
Confirm gross-margin treatment of app-store fees
If 15–30% platform fees are not in COGS, the 79% gross margin and 6:1 LTV/CAC are overstated.
8
Correct the §1033 framing

9
Confirm the definitive raise amount
Both “$20M” and “$22M” appear in the data room; runway at $20M may be shorter than presented.
