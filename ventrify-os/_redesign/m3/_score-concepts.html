Score graphic — six concepts

The score is the most looked-at object in both products. Today it is a number, a bar and a pill: it states a result but carries none of the reasoning that produced it.

Every concept below renders from the real contract — composite, potential, band tone, seven weighted categories, thirty-five signals — so each is a working graphic, not a picture of one. Each is shown at three real states so you can see it hold up: 80 Investment ready, 55 Promising, 34 High risk. Use the Preview switcher to check both identities.
