Making it add up

A score of 80 with seven bars beside it that sum to something else is worse than no breakdown at all — the reader does the arithmetic, gets a different answer, and stops trusting the number.

The x/5 figures do not reconcile, and they fail quietly: at 80 they happen to sum to exactly 80.0, so it looks correct; at 55 they sum to 52.9 against a headline of 55. What does reconcile is each category's contribution — its weight × its score — because that is literally how the composite is built. One honest wrinkle remains: the headline is that sum rounded, so MoneyGym's parts total 54.5 and the headline reads 55. The total row says so rather than pretending otherwise. Shown at 80 and 55.
