Making the dial bigger

The dial is small because the chip and the headroom line sit beside it, taking roughly half the column. Four ways to give that width back.

All four stack the /100 under the numeral, which is what lets the number grow. Measured dial diameter is printed under each so the gain is a number, not an impression. Today's is 200px. Rendered with the real component and the real verdict beside it — switch brands to check both.
