Templates

Templates & UI shell

Every workspace screen is the same thing: one shared UI shell wrapped around one content pattern. The shell — navigation drawer, header, content region — never changes between pages; only what fills the content region does. Document the shell once and every template inherits it, so a change to the chrome rolls through the whole product.

bolt
The drawer and masthead you see below are rendered by the same ds.js functions — drawer() and masthead() — that build the live workspace pages. This page isn't a picture of the shell; it is the shell, documented from source.

Shell states

The drawer has two widths and the whole shell has two themes. Same markup, driven by a class on .m3-app and on the root element — nothing forks.

Drawer ↔ rail
Expanded drawer.m3-app
Collapsed rail.m3-app.rail · 88px

Two further states are automatic: below 840px the drawer detaches (a mobile pattern, not shown here), and the Light / Dark toggle re-tints every token live — try it in the sidebar.

Header patterns

The top of the content region is one of two things. Pick by the page's job: a working list uses the plain app bar; a single subject you're studying gets the tinted masthead so the venture sits on its own surface.

A · Plain app bar — .m3-appbar
Eyebrow
Headline here
Subheading here — sits directly on the page surface, no tint. Eyebrow + headline + one-line subheading, optional action on the right.

Used by Portfolio and Action queue — screens that survey many things at once.

B · Tinted masthead — .masthead

A full-bleed band on --masthead-bg (a subtle violet tint, distinct from the neutral drawer). Carries the subject's identity: back-breadcrumb + actions, the venture header (avatar · name · status), and the tab bar. Used by the Assessment (with tabs) and the Research drill-down (slim, back-nav only). Because it's full-bleed, .m3-main padding drops to zero and an inner .content wrapper re-adds the gutters below the band.
