Replacing the valuation chart

Today's Market hero draws an 8px track with a green band, a red overshoot and an unlabelled tick. It has no axis, so the one number that matters — how far over the ask is — has to be read from the caption. Five replacements.

Read the data line under each concept first. I checked what the live adapter actually emits before designing: asked is parsed out of the ask string, defensibleLo/Hi are regex-matched out of the valuation note and are null when the note doesn't phrase them as "$A–$B pre", multiple is usually null (the comment in adapter.js says the runner emits none, so it is suppressed rather than faked), and comps carry names and prose but no numbers. A concept that needs more than that cannot ship today, and I have said so rather than drawing it with invented figures. Every concept is also rendered in the two degraded states at the bottom.
