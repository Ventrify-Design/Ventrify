Arc × stability

The arc says where the score landed. The spread says how much to trust it. Three ways of putting both in one object.

Same 270° gauge in all three: the solid sweep is the score, the faint continuation is the headroom. What differs is how the three blind draws are expressed. Shown at 80 (draws within 3 — solid), 55 (spread 12 — soft), 34 (spread 15 — soft), and a fourth column with no draws recorded, which is what today's snapshots actually contain.
