Replacing the team chart

Today's Team hero recycles progressLockup: a giant 2, six 7px pips, and a caption that repeats the chip above it. The numeral is the weakest thing on the tab — it reads as a score of 2 while the fact beside it says 3.0 / 5, and what it actually counts is how many people we could look up, which is diligence hygiene, not team quality. Six replacements.

Read the data line under each concept first. I checked the runner's contract before designing. Per person it emits exactly {name, role, background, signal} — no equity, no tenure, no dates, so the sharpest fact on this tab ("two co-founders hold 43% and neither is verifiable") cannot be drawn today without an additive field. gaps are free-text sentences, not role names. What is guaranteed is the rubric: team is five fixed sub-signals (ceo_vision, talent_attraction, founder_market_fit, execution_record, ethics_trust), each scored 0 / 0.5 / 1, and it carries the heaviest weight in the whole rubric at 25%. Every concept is also drawn in the three states a real run lands in.
