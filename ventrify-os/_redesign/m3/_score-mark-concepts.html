The score, kept in view

The investability score is the point of the whole assessment, and today it is only visible on two of six tabs. These put a small, permanent mark in the masthead's tab row so it travels with you — quoting the dial rather than restating the lockup.

Two constraints shaped all six. First, .m3-tabs is full-bleed and overflow-x:auto — at 390px the tabs scroll sideways, so a mark placed inside that container would scroll off the screen. Every concept here sits in a sibling that does not scroll. Second, the masthead minifies on scroll (--mh-p): the hero collapses and the tab row stays, which is exactly why this row is the right home for something that must always be visible. Each concept is shown at rest, minified, at 390px, and with the three score states a real run produces.

The brand switcher now lives in the left drawer (and bottom-left on pages like this one that have no shell), so nothing floats over the corner these marks occupy.
