How Assess Works

Deck in. Decision out.
Five steps, one Workspace.

Drop in the pitch deck and a zip of the data room. A fleet of senior investor-lens agents runs the pipeline — every claim traced back to a cited source — and pipes it all into one coherent verdict.

Step 1 · Ingest

Deck & data room

Upload the pitch deck and a zip of the data room. We read every file — financial model, SAFEs, cap table, customer contracts — and the live website.

Step 2 · Research

Deep web research

Market analysis, competitor landscape, demand & traction and unit economics — web-researched and fully cited. The L3 evidence the score traces to.

Step 3 · Diligence

Investor-lens checks

A competitive benchmark vs named rivals, TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, founder & team diligence, and comparable funding rounds — each handed to the verdict.

Step 4 · Score

Investability score

Thirty-five signals across seven categories — market, team, product, moat, financial, execution, evidence — the quantification of the same research.

Step 5 · Decide

The deal memo

Recommendation, conviction, the ask, the valuation read, the kill-risks, the bull and bear — one decision-grade memo an investor can act on.
