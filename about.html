Why We Exist

Great ideas fail for preventable reasons.

These weren’t isolated cases — they were patterns, and almost all were preventable.

Fragmented Execution

Strategy, design, and development handled by separate teams with no single owner. By launch, the vision was diluted.

Enterprise Pricing

Large retainers, long timelines, heavy overhead. Runway burned before meaningful traction could take hold.

Incomplete Launches

No user validation, no acquisition plan, no iteration strategy. Functional products — but fragile ones.

“The problem wasn’t the founders. It was a model never designed for them.”
