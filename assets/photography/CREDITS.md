# Photography provenance

## The mountain set
`ventrify-*`, `venturestack-*`, `verdictscore-*`, `shine-*` — generated for Ventrify.
One idea sixteen times: a figure on a summit. That is deliberate for the hero,
the doors and the scenes, and it is the reason the texture set below exists.

## The texture set
`texture-*` — free stock from **Pexels**, used under the
[Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which permits commercial use
without attribution. Credited here anyway, because an asset whose origin is not
written down is an asset nobody can re-licence, replace or defend later.

Each was chosen to survive the pane treatment — `mix-blend-mode: color` onto
the brand accent at .55, then a dim. MEASURED on a contact sheet before
selection: dark, low-key, high-contrast frames keep their shape and read as
part of the house world; bright flat ones turn to mush and literal scenes with
people and objects stay visibly stock no matter how hard they are duotoned.

⚠ One frame was replaced after it was already in place: pexels 5491019 put the
word "WAY" across the card in 60px letters. A legible word nobody wrote is a
caption the page did not ask for, and the duotone does not hide type.

Sourced at 1600px, centre-cropped to the library's 2.43:1, encoded at 720 /
1200 / 1600 with `cwebp -q 82`.

⚠ AND BRIGHTNESS-CAPPED, which is not a taste pass. MEASURED mean luminance:
the mountain library sits in a tight band, 84–121 with a mean of 103, because
it was all shot to one brief. Stock is not: this set arrived spanning 7 to 172.
`texture-stack` at 172 put the sixth step's copy on bright paper and failed
photo-contrast at 3.2:1 between 901 and 1100px, where the column narrows and
the copy wraps a line further down. Anything above the library's own mean is
scaled to it (stack ×0.60, crack ×0.82, index ×0.87, paper ×0.89, tear ×0.97);
anything below is left alone, because dark is both safe for text and correct
for the identity — `texture-dial` at 6.6 is a lit dial on near-black and
brightening it would destroy the only thing in the frame.
