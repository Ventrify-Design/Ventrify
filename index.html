Questions

Frequently asked

The questions founders ask before we start working together.

What does working with Ventrify actually look like? +

From the moment you reach out, here’s exactly what the journey looks like:

  1. Discovery Call — We schedule an initial kick-off call. Before that, you’ll complete a short Venture Brief capturing your idea, goals, target market, and what you want to build.
  2. Scope of Work — We review your brief and return with a detailed Scope of Work: what we’ll build, how, and the timeline. Once you sign off, we’re locked in.
  3. First Payment & Discovery + Define — You pay the first instalment (40%) and we begin immediately. The Discover and Define phases run together — market research, opportunity validation, and full product vision. This phase ends with a structured presentation covering research findings, product vision, and a clearly defined MVP scope. You sign off before anything is designed or built.
  4. Second Payment & Design — You pay the second instalment (30%) and we begin everything visual — UX flows, wireframes, pixel-perfect UI, and design system.
  5. Design Presentation & Feedback — We walk you through all design deliverables — product screens and the full design language. You get one structured round of feedback before we lock designs and move into build.
  6. Build & Delivery — We build your MVP to the agreed specification. On delivery, you receive your codebase, Figma files, and all assets — and the final instalment (30%) is due.

Throughout every phase, you’ll always know where we are, what’s coming next, and what’s expected from you. No black boxes, no surprises.

How long does a full venture build take? +

A full 5-phase build typically runs between 10 and 16 weeks from kick-off to delivery.

Discover and Define take roughly 2–3 weeks. Design takes 3–5 weeks. Build and Deliver account for the remainder depending on technical complexity.

We’ll give you a precise timeline in your Scope of Work before anything is signed or paid.

Do I need to be technical to work with Ventrify? +

Not at all. Most of our clients come to us with an idea and a vision — not a technical background. That’s exactly the gap we’re designed to fill.

We handle every layer of the build and communicate everything in plain language. If you do have a technical background, we’ll meet you at that level too.

What do I actually own when the project is complete? +

Everything. Full intellectual property transfers to you upon final delivery and payment. You receive:

  • Complete codebase via private GitHub repositories
  • All Figma design files and brand assets
  • Live app on both app stores
  • Marketing website
  • Design system documentation site
  • All PDF deliverables

No licensing fees, no lock-in, no ongoing dependency on Ventrify.

Can the scope change once the project has started? +

The MVP scope is locked at the end of the Define phase (Gate 2) via a signed Statement of Work. This prevents scope creep and protects both sides. If meaningful change is needed mid-project, we assess openly, communicate timeline and cost impact, and agree a path forward before proceeding.

The Venture Pro retainer (3 months included) provides per month:

  • 4 new blog posts

A master retainer log ensures no content is ever repeated. Additional months can be purchased as an add-on on any tier.

How do payments work? +

All tiers use the same 40/30/30 milestone structure:

  • 40% upfront — before Phase 1 begins
  • 30% at design start — before Phase 3
  • 30% on delivery — before Phase 5 handoff

Invoices are sent via Stripe. No work begins on any phase until the corresponding milestone is paid.
