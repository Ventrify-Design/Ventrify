## THE PROCESS (5-Phase Methodology)

**Section Title:** The Ventrify Methodology

**Subtitle:** A structured 5-phase journey from raw idea to delivered, market-ready venture. Two client gates ensure alignment. Three payment milestones keep things fair. Five parallel workstreams maximise speed.

### Phase 01 — Discover
We deeply research your market, competitors, and target users — building the evidence base that shapes every decision from here.

**Deliverables:**
- Market Research & Analysis (TAM/SAM/SOM, growth trends, Why Now?)
- Competitor Analysis (feature matrix, pricing, acquisition channels, App Store sentiment)
- User Insights (behaviour analysis, community sentiment, willingness to pay, adoption barriers)
- SWOT Analysis
- Market Opportunities Report
- **Gate 1 Research Presentation Deck (PDF)** — presented to you for sign-off

### Phase 02 — Define
With insights in place, we shape the product foundation — who it's for, what it does, how it's positioned, and how it goes to market.

**Deliverables:**
- User Personas (3 detailed personas with goals, frustrations, quotes)
- Product Vision Document
- MVP Feature Prioritisation (MoSCoW framework)
- Tech Stack Recommendation
- User Journeys (step-by-step flows with edge cases)
- Product Roadmap (v1, v1.1, v2)
- Tone of Voice Guide
- Social Media Strategy
- 30-Day Content Calendar
- **4 Presentation Decks auto-generated:**
  - Research & Analysis Deck
  - Product Vision Deck
  - Marketing Strategy Deck
  - Investor Pitch Deck
- **Gate 2 Vision Presentation Deck (PDF)** — scope lock sign-off
- **Statement of Work (PDF)** — formal scope agreement

### Phase 03 — Design
Every screen is specified, every component documented, and the design system is built in Figma with custom plugins.

**Deliverables:**
- User Stories (covering all MVP features)
- Information Architecture (full screen map, navigation tree, data model)
- Wireframe Notes (layout descriptions for every screen)
- Figma Variables Plugin (82 design tokens — colours, typography, spacing, radius, sizing)
- Figma Components Plugin (121 component variants — buttons, inputs, cards, toggles, etc.)
- Component Specification Documents

### Phase 04 — Develop
We build the MVP to production-grade standards — a real app, not a prototype.

**Deliverables:**
- Expo (React Native) mobile app — one codebase, iOS + Android
- Theme system with light/dark mode (all brand tokens)
- Navigation architecture (auth → onboarding → main tabs → settings)
- 11+ shared UI components
- 30+ screens with loading, empty, error, and populated states
- Email authentication (sign up, sign in, forgot password, account deletion)
- Onboarding flow with speech milestone screener (or equivalent for the product)
- Push notifications with category preferences
- PostHog analytics integration
- App Tracking Transparency (iOS) + Android consent
- Demo mode for wireframe screenshots and investor demos
- **Wireframe Deck (PDF)** — real app screenshots in phone frames
- Firebase security rules (production-ready)
- Environment configuration (.env)
- QA checklist (authentication, every screen, navigation, input, network, push notifications, device testing)
- App Store compliance checklist (both platforms)

### Phase 05 — Deliver
Clean, complete handoff. Everything organised, documented, and ready for you to own.

**Deliverables:**
- Handoff Documentation (setup, architecture, deployment)
- Launch Day Playbook (pre-launch, launch day, post-launch checklists)
- 3 GitHub repositories (app, website, design system)
- App Store submission (iOS App Store + Google Play)
- Firebase project transferred to your account
- Sanity CMS access for blog self-management
- Client portal updated with all final links
- Retainer log initialised (Venture Pro)

## CLIENT EXPERIENCE

**Section Title:** What working with Ventrify looks like

### Step 1 — Discovery Call
We discuss your idea, goals, and ambitions. If we're a fit, you complete the Venture Brief — a structured intake form covering your project's scope, market, and priorities.

### Step 2 — Project Setup
We set up your project: brief intake, brand kit (yours or we generate one), payment schedule, and your client portal. You receive a Welcome Pack PDF with everything you need to know about the engagement.

### Step 3 — Research & Sign-Off (Phase 1 + Gate 1)
We conduct deep market research, competitor analysis, and user insights. We present our findings in a branded Research Deck. You review and approve before we move forward.

### Step 4 — Define & Scope Lock (Phase 2 + Gate 2)
We define personas, product vision, MVP features, tech stack, roadmap, and marketing strategy. You receive 4 presentation decks and a Gate 2 summary. Once approved, we generate a Statement of Work — this is the scope lock. Changes to Must Have features after this point require a formal change request.

### Step 5 — Design, Build & Workstreams (Phases 3-4)
Design documentation feeds into development. The MVP app is built. In parallel, the marketing website, social content, blog posts, video brief, and design system are produced. You have real-time visibility via the client portal.

### Step 6 — Deliver & Launch (Phase 5)
You receive: 3 GitHub repos, live app on both stores, Figma files, marketing site, design system, all PDFs, and a Launch Day Playbook coordinating the go-live moment. Full IP transfers to you.

**Throughout every phase, you'll always know where we are, what's coming next, and what's expected from you. No black boxes, no surprises.**

## FAQ

### What does working with Ventrify actually look like?
You start with a discovery call, then complete our Venture Brief intake form. We set up your project, generate a Welcome Pack, and begin Phase 1 (Research). Every phase ends with a client gate — a branded presentation deck where you review and approve our findings before we proceed. After Gate 2, the scope is locked via a Statement of Work. We build the app, website, and all marketing materials in parallel, with real-time progress visible in your client portal. Upon delivery, everything transfers to you — code, design files, services, and full IP.

### How long does a full venture build take?
Typically 10–16 weeks from kick-off to delivery. Discover and Define: 2–3 weeks. Design: 2–3 weeks. Build and parallel workstreams: 4–8 weeks depending on complexity. Deliver: 1 week.

### Do I need to be technical to work with Ventrify?
Not at all. Most clients come with an idea and a vision — not a technical background. That's the gap we fill. We handle every layer from strategy through launch. You'll never need to touch a terminal, write code, or configure a server.

### What do I actually own when the project is complete?
Everything. Full intellectual property transfers to you upon final delivery and payment. You receive the complete codebase via private GitHub repositories, all Figma design files, brand assets, a live app on both app stores, a marketing website, a design system documentation site, and all PDF deliverables. No licensing fees, no lock-in, no ongoing dependency on Ventrify.

### Can the scope change once the project has started?
The MVP scope is locked at the end of the Define phase (Gate 2) via a signed Statement of Work. This prevents scope creep and protects both sides. If meaningful change is needed mid-project, we assess openly, communicate timeline and cost impact, and agree a path forward before proceeding.

### Which tier is right for me?
**Launchpad** if you need a validated strategy and a working MVP — the foundation to start testing and iterating. **Venture** if you also need to go to market — website, investor deck, social media, and blog content ready for launch day. **Venture Pro** if you want the complete package including video production and 3 months of post-launch content retainer with monthly analytics reports.

### What's included in the retainer?
The Venture Pro retainer (3 months included) provides: 4 new blog posts, 12 new social media posts, 1 product update email, and 1 analytics report per month. A master retainer log ensures no content is ever repeated. Additional months can be purchased as an add-on on any tier.

### How do payments work?
All tiers use the same 40/30/30 milestone structure: 40% upfront (before Phase 1 begins), 30% at design start (before Phase 3), and 30% on delivery (before Phase 5 handoff). Invoices are generated and sent via Stripe. No work begins on any phase until the corresponding milestone is paid.
