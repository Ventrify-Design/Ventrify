FAQ

Common questions

Pricing clarity, payment terms, scope, and IP — answered.

Why do you publish your pricing? +

Because guessing games waste everyone’s time. If our pricing doesn’t fit your stage, you should know in 60 seconds — not after three discovery calls.

Every tier lists exactly what’s included and what it costs. The starting price is what you’ll pay for the defined scope. If you need something outside that scope, we quote it separately as an add-on.

What does “starting from” actually mean? +

The listed price is the minimum for that tier’s defined scope — an MVP with a standard feature set, a marketing site with standard pages, a standard research pack.

If your venture needs more (complex integrations, multi-market research, extra user types, or native features beyond the standard scope), we quote the uplift transparently before you sign. The “starting from” figure is never a bait price.

How do payments work? +

All tiers use the same 40/30/30 milestone structure:

  • 40% upfront — before Phase 1 begins
  • 30% at design start — after research is presented and MVP scope is locked
  • 30% on delivery — before final handover

Invoices are sent via Stripe. No work begins on any phase until the corresponding milestone is paid.

What happens with IP and ownership? +

You own everything. 100% IP transfer on delivery — code, designs, brand assets, research, and documentation. No recurring licence fees, no vendor lock-in, no strings.

All code lives in your GitHub organisation. All Figma files are transferred to your team. We keep zero equity and claim zero ongoing rights.

Can I add services to any tier? +

Yes. Every add-on listed above can be bolted onto any tier, or purchased standalone. We quote them transparently and include them in the same Statement of Work.

If you need something that isn’t listed (a custom integration, a specific partnership campaign, extended user research), we’ll quote it during the discovery call.

What’s the 3-month content retainer in Venture Pro? +

Post-launch, we keep producing content to feed your marketing engine. Each month includes:

  • 4 new blog posts
  • 12 new social media posts
  • 1 product update email
  • 1 analytics report

A master retainer log ensures no content is ever repeated. Additional months can be purchased as an add-on on any tier.

Not sure which tier fits. What should I do? +

Book a discovery call. We’ll walk through your stage, goals, and budget, and recommend the tier (and any add-ons) that best fit your venture. If none of the tiers fit cleanly, we’ll tell you — and point you somewhere that will.
