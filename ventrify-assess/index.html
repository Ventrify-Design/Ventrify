FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does an assessment take?+

Hours, not days. You upload the deck and data room, and the full memo — score, research, benchmark and verdict — comes back the same day.

How accurate is it — can I actually rely on it?+

Every claim is researched against live sources and footnoted, so you can check the working. It's built to be even-handed and stage-calibrated rather than optimistic — and it's explicit about what it couldn't verify. It's decision support, not a substitute for your judgement.

What files can I upload?+

Pitch decks, financial models and spreadsheets, and documents — including scanned and signed legal files (cap tables, agreements, incorporation docs) that most tools silently drop. If a document can't be read, it's flagged for review, never scored as missing.

Is it just an AI summary of the deck?+

No. It does the work an associate would — live web research, a rebuilt market size, comparable rounds, founder checks against public record, and a weighted score — then writes a memo with a recommendation you can defend.

What is the Investability Score?+

A weighted 0–100 composite across seven dimensions and 35 sub-signals, stage-calibrated so an early idea and a Series A are judged on the right bar. The radar also shows the venture's potential — where it could reach by closing the gaps the memo identifies.

How does licensing work?+

Verdict is licensed annually to your organisation — the same model as Ventrify OS — with member or team seats and the same rigorous bar on every deal. Higher tiers add your own rubric weights, white-label and API. Final pricing is on enquiry.
