How it works & The output — three treatments

Both sections render from the same <v-steps> component, so each treatment is shown on both lists. A fix that only works on one would put two systems in one page. The real stylesheets are linked, so these are the page's own type, colour and spacing.

Treatment 1

The ledger

No boxes and no photographs — rules, numerals and text. The product's whole claim is that it shows its working, and a ruled ledger is what working looks like. It also lets the second list be what it actually is: a table of contents.

How it works

  1. 01

    Upload the data room

    Bring together decks, financial models, spreadsheets, contracts and other supporting documents.

  2. 02

    Review readiness

    Before assessment, receive a review of potential gaps and missing supporting material so you can strengthen the data room.

  3. 03

    Examine the evidence

    Specialist analysis steps review claims, markets, competitors, comparables, assumptions and risks.

  4. 04

    Freeze the findings

    Evidence is fixed before scoring so the basis for the assessment can be identified.

  5. 05

    Score against criteria

    Blind readers apply defined criteria and anchored thresholds; disagreement is surfaced rather than hidden.

  6. 06

    Review the memo

    Receive a structured assessment with reasoning and source references to support further diligence.

The output

  1. 01

    Overall score and stated guidance

  2. 02

    Investment thesis with supporting and opposing evidence

  3. 03

    Claims marked as supported, unverified or contradicted

  4. 04

    Market, competitor and comparable analysis

  5. 05

    Key assumptions and risks that could change the decision

  6. 06

    Evidence references and a record of material gaps

The rail

One line threading all six, so the sequence is drawn rather than numbered. It is the only treatment that says this happens, then this — which is the thing a grid of six cards cannot say. On the contents list the rail becomes a spine down the side of the document.

Treatment 3

Quiet panes

The smallest change: the card shape and the three-across grid stay, the photographs go. Everything the textures were doing is done by one tinted surface and a rule. Keeps the page's existing rhythm — and the contents cards stop being a sentence above a void.

Treatment 6

Instruments

The direct answer to the complaint: if a stock photograph says nothing about the product, show the product. Each entry carries a small, real fragment of the thing it describes — the coverage bar, the citation, the frozen hash, the dial, the claim tags. Drawn in CSS, so there is no image weight and nothing to license. The most work of the six, and the only one where the picture is evidence rather than decoration.

Treatment 7 — the refinement

The ledger, with chapter numerals

Treatment 1's layout, treatment 4's numbers, and a sentence under every output entry — which is the change that makes both sections genuinely one component rather than two that resemble each other. The numerals flow down each column, so the sequence continues the way it would in a book instead of zig-zagging across the page.
