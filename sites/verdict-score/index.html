The challenge

Investment decisions need more than a deck and a feeling.

VerdictScore gives investment teams a consistent first pass, with findings, criteria and reasoning they can inspect.

How it works

From data room to a judgement you can inspect.

The workflow is designed to make each stage visible, from source documents to the final assessment.

  • Upload the data room

    Bring together decks, financial models, spreadsheets, contracts and other supporting documents.

  • Review readiness

    Before assessment, receive a review of potential gaps and missing supporting material so you can strengthen the data room.

  • Examine the evidence

    Specialist analysis steps review claims, markets, competitors, comparables, assumptions and risks.

  • Freeze the findings

    Evidence is fixed before scoring so the basis for the assessment can be identified.

  • Score against criteria

    Blind readers apply defined criteria and anchored thresholds; disagreement is surfaced rather than hidden.

  • Review the memo

    Receive a structured assessment with reasoning and source references to support further diligence.

    Reproducibility

    The same evidence, scored three times, blind.

    Every score is drawn three times. Three independent reads work the frozen evidence without seeing each other, and what they do with it is published alongside the number.
