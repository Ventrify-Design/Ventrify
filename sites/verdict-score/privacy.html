Privacy

What we collect when you submit a venture for review, why we collect it, who sees it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 25 November 2026

VerdictScore reads a pitch deck and, if you share one, a data room, and returns a written view of how investment-ready the venture looks. To do that we have to hold your documents and your contact details for a while. This page says exactly what that means.

The short version. We keep your deck and use it to produce your review. We do not sell it, we do not use it to train any model, and if you submitted through an investor's website that investor sees your result — never your deck or your data room. Ask us and we will delete everything.

Who we are

VerdictScore is a product of Ventrify. If you want to reach a person about anything on this page, use the contact form on the VerdictScore site and say it is a privacy request — it reaches us directly.

What we collect

What you give us

  • Your name, work email and company name.
  • The stage you select.
  • Your pitch deck, as a file.
  • A data room link, if you choose to share one, and the documents we can read through it.

What we record automatically

  • Your IP address and browser user-agent at the moment you submit.
  • Which partner's website you submitted from, if you came through one.
  • A one-way hash of your email address, used only to tell whether that address has submitted recently. It cannot be turned back into your address.

The IP address and the hash exist for one reason: a free service on a public form is otherwise trivial to abuse, and each review costs us real money to produce. They are abuse limits, not analytics.

What we create

  • Your review — the band, the findings, and what they were based on.
  • If you buy a full assessment, the assessment and its deal memo.
  • A record of which public sources we consulted about your market and competitors.

Why, and on what basis

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, our basis is performing the service you asked for for everything in the first two rows, and our legitimate interest in not having a free service abused for the third.

Who sees it

The investor whose site you used

If you submitted through an investor's website — an angel group, a fund — that investor receives your review result. That is the point of it being on their site, and the form says so before you submit.

They do not receive your pitch deck or your data room. This is not a policy we ask you to trust: the permission rules on our database refuse those documents to a partner account. If you did not come through a partner, nobody outside Ventrify sees anything.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the list above and the investor described in the previous section.

We do not train models on your venture

Your documents are sent to Anthropic's API so the model can read them and write your review. They are not used to train any model — not ours, and not Anthropic's, whose API terms exclude submitted data from training.

Research about your market

Producing a review involves searching public sources about your market and your competitors. Those searches are about the sector, not about you personally, and we do not publish anything about your venture anywhere.

How long we keep it

We keep your documents and your results for as long as your workspace exists, so that you can go back and read them. We do not currently delete them on a fixed schedule.

If you want them gone, ask and we will delete them — your deck, your data room contents, your results and your account. Use the contact form and say so. We will do it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to object to our using it. Ask through the contact form and we will answer.

If you are in the UK or the EU and you think we have handled this badly, you can complain to your data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Where your information is held

Our providers operate internationally, so your information may be processed outside the country you live in, including in the United States.

Changes

If we change what we collect or who sees it, we will change this page and move the date at the top. If the change is significant and we have your email, we will tell you.
